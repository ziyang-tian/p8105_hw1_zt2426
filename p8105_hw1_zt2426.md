mean() works for the numeric and logical columns. For the logical
column, R counts TRUE as 1 and FALSE as 0, so its mean is the proportion
of values above zero. The character and factor columns return NA because

``` r
as.numeric(pull(homework_df, above_zero))
as.numeric(pull(homework_df, character_values))
as.numeric(pull(homework_df, factor_values))
```

With as.numeric(), logical values become 1 and 0. The letters become NA
because they cannot be read as numbers. The factor becomes 1, 2, or 3
according to its level order. These are category codes, so taking their
mean would not have a useful interpretation.
